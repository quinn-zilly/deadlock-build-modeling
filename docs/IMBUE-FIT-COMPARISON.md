# Does imbue belong in the archetype clustering?

Generated by `scripts/compare_imbue_fits.py`. The decision is ADR 0001.

Three fits of the same purchase table in one run. `families` uses build
family shares alone. `imbue` is the rejected first attempt, which adds
imbue target shares, `has_imbue`, and an imbue count. `conditional`
adds target shares only, with players who imbued nothing placed at
their hero's mean.

The separating item is the item with the biggest pick-rate gap between
the fit's two least distinct clusters. An asterisk marks one of the
nine imbueable items. `split` is what the conditional block did to the
hero's split compared with build families alone.

## Verdict

- Splits gained compared with build families alone: Abrams, Apollo, Dynamo, McGinnis, Mo & Krill, Rem, Shiv, Sinclair, The Doorman, Victor
- Splits **lost** compared with build families alone: Billy, Drifter, Haze, Holliday, Paradox, Venator, Vyper, Warden
- Splits lost compared with the rejected first attempt: Apollo, Bebop, Billy, Calico, Celeste, Drifter, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Mirage, Paradox, Silver, Venator, Vindicta, Viscous, Wraith, Yamato
- Separating item is imbueable: 5 of 31 split heroes (16%) with build families alone, 27 of 33 (82%) with the first attempt, and 22 of 28 (79%) with the conditional block. The build-family number is the control: it shows how often an imbueable item separates a hero's builds without the block.

**No hero loses a split: NO. Share drops by at least half: NO.**

By the rule set before the results, imbue stays out of the clustering and is used only for naming and advice.
